# Findings, 2026-11-18

Research run `/tspu-research`, scope: all chapters (no arguments). Second run. The chapters have not changed since the first run, so their claims are as recorded in [findings-2026-10-28.md](findings-2026-10-28.md). This file records only what is new or changed since that run. Section numbers below match the first findings file and the chapters.

Working input for chapter revisions, not documentation. Sources are cited by their id in [sources.md](sources.md); this run added S177–S208. Each finding gives the chapter's current claim, what the newer sources say, the date it applies to, the sources and a confidence, under a verdict: **confirms**, **updates** or **contradicts**. Where a finding moves an earlier one, it names that section of the 2026-10-28 file.

How the run worked:
- **Re-checks:** the net4people threads (GitHub API, every issue updated since 2026-10-28), the zapret readme, the Zenodo versions of S044, the ЦМУ ССОП portal (reports, news, documents), the RKN subsidy file, the zakupki search and RKN's own news list.
- **New material:** searches in Russian and English for 2026-10-28 → 2026-11-18, plus older items that answer open questions from the first run. Those are marked "published earlier, new to the register" in sources.md.
- **Reading:** every source cited here was opened. Where only a headline or description rendered, the register says so.

Ground rules kept: public material only; nothing leaked was opened; the vendor documents are references, not a content source.

## Summary

1. **Machine learning in АСБИ (2026-11-13).** Forbes read RKN's digital-transformation programme passport for 2026–2028. It reports that RKN spent 2.27 bn ₽ on "filtering mechanisms using machine learning", built into АСБИ and "complementing" ТСПУ, on an unnamed domestic model running on RKN's own infrastructure. Новая газета Европа read the same passport differently: «интеллектуальная поддержка принятия решений» for 101 RKN staff, not per-connection filtering. The first run had this only as a plan (22.3). (overview, protocol-blocking)
2. **«Спектр-2026», the 25th forum (Interfax reports Смирнов speaking on 22 October).** RKN deputy head О. Терляков said the national anti-DDoS system (НСПА) is «развернутая на технических средствах противодействия угрозам (ТСПУ)». В. Смирнов, now director of ЦМУ ССОП, said ТСПУ can absorb up to 160 Tbit/s of inbound attack traffic. He also said ЦМУ ССОП plans to «сформировать эшелон защиты при помощи ТСПУ» against attack sources inside Russia, the first public use of «эшелон» I have found (see 4). No new ТСПУ count was given. (overview, echelon, central-management)
3. **Address bans driven by detection.** A VPN developer (interested party) and forum reports describe server-IP and whole-subnet bans that follow protocol detection in 2026. That is close to the chapters' two-stage model, which the first run had mostly contradicted (22.2–22.5). (protocol-blocking)
4. **Whitelists.** The minister puts the size limit of the list (about 900 resources) on «технические ограничения для операторов связи». A practitioner measurement finds permission granted per IP prefix and varying by operator and region. This leans the open question towards operator-side, prefix-based enforcement. (filter-dpi 17.4.3)
5. **Order № 51 and Континент.** ГРЧЦ's own 2025 instruction for the order № 51 address-reporting interface requires АПКШ «Континент» (КС3) for the operator's channel to ЦМУ ССОП. Enforcement: 85 operators fined by 2026-05-21. (overview 1.2, management-segment 20.3, central-management 21.2)
6. **НСДИ as a blocking tool.** On 2026-02-10/11, YouTube, web WhatsApp and at least 11 other domains were removed from the national DNS. This is a blocking method outside ТСПУ that the chapters never mention. (uncovered)
7. **Budget and the foreign-traffic cap are still open.** The 2027–2029 budget bill was recommended for second reading on 2026-11-17 and has not been adopted. The foreign-traffic cap through ТСПУ is still a proposal for a telecom-reform bill. (overview 1.2–1.4)

#### 1.2 Law: the S044-only items, re-checked
- **33-ФЗ of 20.02.2026: confirms** S044's description, now from the primary text. Operators must «приостановить оказание услуг связи при поступлении требований от органов федеральной службы безопасности в случаях, установленных нормативными правовыми актами Президента», and they are not liable for doing so (ст. 46 п. 1, ст. 68 п. 6.1). The law does not mention ТСПУ: the duty to suspend falls on the operator's own network. **Sources:** S178. **Confidence:** high (primary text). **Moves:** 1.2 of 2026-10-28, from medium to high.
- **RKN order № 51 of 28.02.2025: confirms, with detail.**
  - **Title.** ГРЧЦ's connection instruction quotes the order's full title and its п. 1 подп. «г» (subscriber IPv4 and IPv6 addresses).
  - **Enforcement.** In March 2026 RKN sent notices to 1,359 operators, and by 21 May 85 had been fined.
  - **Content.** The data include where each address is used, by municipality, and the identifiers of the ТСПУ the traffic passes through. A changed address is reported within a day, or within an hour on RKN's request. RKN says it collects addresses assigned to equipment, not which subscriber holds them.

  **Sources:** S177, S186. **Confidence:** high for the title (S177); medium for the enforcement figures (RKN via Известия, via Meduza). **Moves:** 1.2 of 2026-10-28, from medium to medium-high.
- **Минцифры order № 219 of 16.03.2026 (satellite traffic through ТСПУ): unchanged.** The primary text was not found. publication.pravo.gov.ru was reachable on the Russian route, but its API ignored the search parameters. It remains an S044-only item.

#### 17.3 Degradation: updates (per-connection packet caps by destination network)
- **Claim:** degradation is a probabilistic drop. The first run found byte caps instead: about 16 KB, or a freeze after about 25 packets, from 2025-06-09.
- **Newer sources:**
  - **A Habr measurement of 5–6.11.2026, as retold.** I could not find the original.
    - Method: 435 endpoints at 154 hosting companies, each reachable from Europe, tested over home and mobile lines in one city.
    - Home: 87% loaded. The failures clustered in 14 networks (Hetzner, DigitalOcean, Vultr, Scaleway, M247; OVH 12 of 12).
    - Thresholds: about 25 packets per TCP connection at home (4.8 KB with small packets, 33 KB with full-size ones); about 8 packets on Beeline mobile and in a small MTS sample.
    - How the freeze looks: both directions stop, but the connection is not reset. The counter is per connection, and UDP is unaffected (AmneziaWG on the same Hetzner server kept working).
    - Scope: it follows the autonomous system. A brand's servers in its own AS worked, while its servers rented inside OVH, Clouvider or Glesys froze.
  - **Forum, 2026-11-15.** From an ER-Telecom line, TCP connects to Hetzner succeed but the TLS handshake is killed, with 0 bytes delivered. Vultr, Linode and Cloudflare were reachable at the same time. This differs from the Habr report on Vultr, possibly because the ISP or the date differs.
  - **Habr, June 2026 (single-source reconstruction).** VLESS+Reality over plain TCP froze after about 16 KB on wired operators from 2026-02-17. The author describes a behaviour model keyed on "suspicious" destination CIDR or ASN plus the client's TLS fingerprint.
- **Verdict:** updates. These reports fit the 2025 byte and packet caps, extend them to more hosting networks and to mobile at a lower threshold, and show the target is the destination network, not the protocol. No measurement shows a probabilistic drop.
- **Applies to:** 2026-02 → 2026-11. **Sources:** S197, S173, S205. **Confidence:** low (a retelling, a forum and a self-declared reconstruction).

#### 17.4.3 Whitelist mode: updates (prefix-based; points at operators)
- **Claim:** whitelist mode is "never used in ТСПУ". The first run left it open whether ТСПУ or the operators' own DPI enforces the mobile whitelists.
- **Newer sources:**
  - **Minister М. Шадаев (2026-11-12).** About 900 resources are on the list, and it «не может быть бесконечным. Здесь есть технические ограничения для операторов связи».
  - **Минцифры (2026-08-31).** It asked providers to «вынести IP-адреса белого списка в отдельные подсети», so that blocked resources cannot use them (headline and description only). A forum summary says the request went to hosting providers, CDNs and web-protection services (S206).
  - **allowlist-probe (Beeline, June and August 2026):**
    - permission is granted per prefix, not per network: one ASN passed on one block and failed on another in the same run;
    - in a paired test, 4 of 28 working endpoints passed over a SIM, all VLESS+Reality with a permitted Russian SNI;
    - lists differ by operator and region.
  - **Forum.** Behaviour can flip on network re-registration within one operator and one city (MTS St Petersburg).
- **Verdict:** updates. Whitelisting is mainly by IP prefix, sometimes with SNI. The minister places the capacity limit with the operators, which leans towards operator-side enforcement. No source names the box.
- **Applies to:** 2026-06 → 2026-11. **Sources:** S198, S189, S207, S206. **Confidence:** medium that matching is by prefix; low on which equipment does it.
- **Moves:** 17.4.3 of 2026-10-28 and the whitelist open question.

### 22. protocol-blocking (docs/protocol-blocking.md)

#### 22.2–22.5, 22.10 The two-stage model (detect, then block by address lists): confirms partly, for 2026
- **Claim:** protocols are not blocked directly on detection. Detections feed cleaned IP+port lists, applied 5–15 minutes later.
- **Newer sources:**
  - **The Amnezia developer, on the campaign that began on 2026-06-01** (an interested party):
    - RKN gathered signatures for AmneziaWG 2.0 for almost a year and released them one at a time;
    - it now «наблюдают за тем, кто использует соединение, вычисляют [IP-]адреса и точечно блокируют серверы и целые подсети»;
    - «Когда они вносят сигнатуру в ТСПУ, он тут же блокирует IP-адреса серверов»;
    - scoring is about 50 criteria, and more than 25 points means a block; traffic volume weighs heavily.
  - **The 2026-08-04 wave.** More than 20 hosting providers were hit; Amnezia, Paper VPN and others posted notices.
  - **Forum, August–November 2026:**
    - mass IP bans on 4 August and 21 September (for example, 5,000 of 30,000 addresses at one provider and 3,000 of 29,000 at another);
    - a VPN exit address banned within about 2 days, and a second within about 25 hours with all inbound ports closed;
    - in one case the ban applied on home networks only.
- **Verdict:** confirms partly. In 2026, address and subnet bans that follow detection are a main method, alongside the in-session signature blocking the first run documented. The observed delays (about 25 h to 2 days for server addresses) are much longer than the chapter's 5–15 minutes. The ban scope now reaches whole subnets. The central "cleaning" step still cannot be seen from outside.
- **Applies to:** 2026-06 → 2026-11. **Sources:** S187, S188, S173, S195. **Confidence:** low-medium.
- **Moves:** 22.2–22.5 of 2026-10-28, which had "delayed, list-like blocking is also seen" from older and weaker evidence; and the two-stage open question.
