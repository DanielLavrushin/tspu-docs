One row per source. Rows are never deleted: when a source disappears, changes or is replaced, its status says so.
New rows get the next free id and go into the section of their type; ids are never reused or renumbered.
Findings files (`findings-YYYY-MM-DD.md`) cite these ids.

Columns: **id**; **title**; **author or publisher**; **date** of publication (YYYY-MM-DD where known);
**URL** as read; **type** (official, research, vendor, journalism, forum); **access** from this machine:
direct, Russia only (worked only through the Russian route of the `ru-route` skill), or unreachable from
both routes; **reliability** note; **last checked**; **status** (current, changed, superseded, gone, noted only, circular).
