# Findings, 2026-10-28

Research run `/tspu-research`, scope: all chapters (no arguments). This is the first run: there was no earlier register or findings file.

Working input for chapter revisions, not documentation. Sources are cited by their id in [sources.md](sources.md). Each finding gives the chapter's current claim, what newer public sources say, the date it applies to, the sources and a confidence, under a verdict: **confirms**, **updates** or **contradicts**.

How the run worked: four parallel research passes (law and organisation; measurement research; hardware and vendors; events 2020–2026), then a verification pass that re-read the key items at the source: the text of the IMC 2022 and FOCI 2026 papers, Коммерсантъ, Kod.ru, Meduza, Habr, The Bell, Fontanka, RG, ГАРАНТ, the RKN subsidy file and the September 2026 University of Amsterdam report (S044). The verification pass corrected several details, and the corrections are included below. Rows that the register marks "seen only in search results" carry little weight here.

Ground rules kept: public material only; leaked material is noted at the end and was not opened; the vendor documents are references for dating and versions, not a content source for the chapters.

## Summary

1. **Scale.** The chapters' «~350 площадок, ~5 000 устройств» is four years old. Official and on-record figures: «более 1250» ТСПУ at the end of 2023, «более 1400 ТСПУ» carrying 132.3 Tbit/s in October 2025, and 100% of mobile, broadband and cross-border nodes covered since August 2023. The plan is 954 Tbit/s by 2030. (overview 1.1, central-management 21.3)
2. **Legal base replaced.** ПП 126 expired on 2025-09-01 and was replaced by ПП 1333. ПП 127 was repealed by ПП 1667 from 2026-03-01. RKN order 228 was replaced by order 25 (2024). The official operator of the central management is ЦМУ ССОП inside ФГУП «ГРЧЦ», which the chapters never name. Operators now have a formal procedure for ТСПУ problems and defined cases in which they may stop routing traffic through ТСПУ. (overview 1.2, central-management 21, troubleshooting 23.7)
3. **Protocol blocking is often immediate, by signature.** The chapters' model is "recognise, log, clean centrally, block by IP+port lists", with direct blocking never applied. That model is contradicted for QUIC (2022), OpenVPN and WireGuard (2023), Shadowsocks and VMess (2024), Snowflake (2021, 2026), ECH (2024) and Telegram proxies (2026). Delayed IP bans also exist. (protocol-blocking 22)
4. **How HTTPS is cut.** Measurements show the server's packets rewritten to RST/ACK (2022) or packets dropped (2026). They do not show RST sent to both sides, and no measurement shows the HTTP 307 stub page. (filter 10.1.5, filter-dpi 17.4.4, traffic-flow 2)
5. **Throttling.** What has been measured is rate policing (Twitter 2021: 130–150 kbit/s), a per-connection cap of about 16 KB (since 2025-06-09) and connection-count policing (2025). No measurement shows the probabilistic packet drop the chapter describes. (filter-dpi 17.3)
6. **QUIC.** All QUIC v1 to foreign destinations was dropped from 2022-03-04. Since 2022–2023 QUIC is blocked by SNI with the same list as TLS. (filter-dpi 17.4.9)
7. **Whitelists.** Mobile networks have run in whitelist-only mode since 2025. Sources disagree on whether ТСПУ or the operators' own DPI implements it. (filter-dpi 17.4.3)
8. **Hardware.**
   - A domestic bypass has been delivered since 2023 and was on several hundred nodes by mid-2024.
   - Silicom has left Russia.
   - The filter platform has been domestic since 2022.
   - Intel ended Tofino (last deliveries 2025-02-28).
   - "Xeon Gold 6212" does not exist; the part is the 6212U. (bypass 5.6, filter-platform 11, balancer-platform 7)
9. **Direction matters.** Only connections opened from inside Russia trigger blocking. (contradicts filter-sessions 12.4)
10. **Placement.** ТСПУ is confirmed close to subscribers, and RKN's 2019 letter recommends the point after BRAS, before NAT. Upstream-only boxes also sit at transit ISPs, and only 493 of 1,981 operators are connected to ТСПУ. The chapters' "type A at most operators" does not hold in that wording. (placement 3, echelon 4)

## Most out-of-date chapters

1. **protocol-blocking (22).** The two-stage IP-list model is now only part of the picture: signature blocking inside the session, throttling, VPN-protocol campaigns, ECH, machine-learning plans.
2. **filter-dpi (17).** The degradation model, the QUIC list, whitelist mode, registry delivery and ECH.
3. **central-management (21) and overview (1).** Scale, ЦМУ ССОП, the 2020 decrees that no longer apply, and the federal ЦСУ still described as "being built".
4. **bypass (5).** The domestic bypass is deployed; Silicom has exited.
5. **filter-platform (11).** CPU and model errors; a domestic platform since 2022; a newer model line.
6. **troubleshooting (23.7).** Formal procedures now exist.
7. **echelon (4).** There is no public trace of «тип Б», the Tofino supply has ended, and IP blocking is seen at transit level.

No public non-vendor sources exist to test against: balancer-config (8), balancer-monitoring (9), filter-cli (13), filter-subsystems (14), filter-interfaces (15), filter-acl-pools (16), filter-monitoring (18), most of traffic-flow (2), management-segment 20.1, 20.2 and 20.4.

#### 1.1 Who runs the centre: updates
- **Claim:** АСБИ is ТСПУ plus a central management system (ЦСУ, «ЦСУО» on diagrams). ЦМУ ССОП is not mentioned.
- **Newer sources:**
  - The operator of centralised management is the Центр мониторинга и управления сетью связи общего пользования (ЦМУ ССОП). It was created under ПП № 136 of 13.02.2019, is a structural unit of ФГУП «ГРЧЦ» and works around the clock.
  - It manages ТСПУ «посредством дистанционного направления программных команд» (Положение о ЦМУ ССОП, RKN order № 225, as amended 24.04.2024). The law (ст. 65.1) gives it the centralised-management measures.
  - RKN (2022): all ТСПУ are run by ЦМУ ССОП.
  - The official name of the central information system is «ИС мониторинга и управления ССОП» (ИС «ЦМУ ССОП»). «ЦСУ» and «ЦСУО» do not appear in the acts read.
  - A 2023 ГРЧЦ contract, as reported, covers «модификация разработанного ПО централизованной системы управления оборудованием ТСПУ», which matches «ЦСУО».
- **Applies to:** 2019–2026. **Sources:** S001, S019, S031, S010, S015, S032, S085. **Confidence:** high for the roles; medium for how the ЦСУ relates to the ИС «ЦМУ ССОП».

#### 1.2 Law 90-ФЗ: confirms; the framework has changed (updates)
- **Claim:** 90-ФЗ of 01.05.2019 introduced ТСПУ; no later acts are named.
- **Newer sources:** 90-ФЗ is in force from 01.11.2019. Status of the later acts in 2026-10:
  - **Installation rules.** ПП 126 of 12.02.2020 «прекратил действие с 1 сентября 2025 г. в связи с истечением срока». It is replaced by ПП 1333 of 30.08.2025, in force 01.09.2025 to 01.09.2031.
  - **Centralised management.** ПП 127 of 12.02.2020 is repealed by ПП 1667 of 27.10.2025, in force 01.03.2026 to 01.03.2032.
  - **Technical conditions.** RKN order № 228 (31.07.2019) is replaced by order № 25 of 19.02.2024: technical conditions of installation and network requirements.
  - **Law «О связи»:**
    - ТСПУ is required on equipment carrying more than 10 Gbit/s (319-ФЗ, from 01.12.2021).
    - Interconnecting operators must route connected ISPs' traffic through ТСПУ (ст. 46 п. 5.2, from 2023; reworded by 216-ФЗ).
    - Owners of traffic exchange points must install ТСПУ (473-ФЗ, 2023).
    - ст. 65.2 allows network management on demand of the Prosecutor General (216-ФЗ of 08.08.2024).
    - Operators are not liable for licence breaches caused by ТСПУ failures.
  - **Liability.** КоАП 13.42 and 13.42.1 (extended to IXP owners on 28.12.2025) and УК 274.2.
  - **RKN order № 51 of 28.02.2025.** Operators report subscriber IP addresses and the identifiers of the ТСПУ their traffic passes through.
  - **Reported only by S044 (not opened here):**
    - 33-ФЗ of 20.02.2026: operators suspend service on FSB demand, without liability.
    - Минцифры order № 219 of 16.03.2026: from 06.07.2026, traffic from 5G handsets in satellite mode must pass through ТСПУ.
- **Applies to:** 2019–2026. **Sources:** S078, S003, S004, S026, S028, S017, S006, S012, S022, S031, S030, S107, S044. **Confidence:** high for the texts read; medium for the items from S044 only.

#### 1.3 "Type A is used practically everywhere": updates (qualifies)
- **Claim:** ТСПУ тип А «используется практически везде». The echelon chapter says it «устанавливается у большинства операторов».
- **Newer sources:**
  - RKN's explanatory note (2025): order 51 «коснётся 1981 оператора…, из которых 493 подключены к ТСПУ».
  - Small ISPs in Tyumen are filtered by ТСПУ inside Rostelecom ("censorship-as-a-service", IMC 2022).
  - The law obliges interconnecting operators to route connected ISPs' traffic through ТСПУ.

  So most operators have no ТСПУ of their own: "everywhere" holds for traffic, not for operators.
- **Applies to:** 2022–2025. **Sources:** S107, S037, S031. **Confidence:** medium.

#### 1.3 Transparency, an L2 "wire": confirms (no L3 hop); updates (fingerprintable)
- **Claim:** ТСПУ is invisible to the operator; filters work at L2 with no L3 interfaces.
- **Newer sources:**
  - **Not an L3 hop.** Measurements place ТСПУ between hop N and N+1, and it never answers probes itself.
  - **Fingerprintable anyway.** It buffers IP fragments, accepts at most 45 and rewrites the TTL of later fragments. Researchers used this to find 1,013,600 endpoints in 650 ASes behind ТСПУ-like devices.
  - **Invisible from outside.** It filters only flows opened inside Russia, so remote DPI fingerprinting does not see it.
- **Applies to:** 2021–2025. **Sources:** S034, S037, S041. **Confidence:** high.

#### 1.3–1.4 What ТСПУ does: updates
- **Claim:** filtering by the RKN registry, protocol recognition, blocking and degradation.
- **Newer sources:**
  - **Official wording.** Budget wording (2026) describes АСБИ as equipment «для фильтрации трафика, блокировки и замедления сайтов и сервисов».
  - **Uses on record:**
    - Twitter slowdown (2021): RKN head on record;
    - ECH (2024): ЦМУ ССОП on record;
    - VPN protocols (2023–2025);
    - Telegram (2026).
  - **Other roles and plans:**
    - anti-DDoS;
    - planned «виртуализации функций на оборудовании ТСПУ» (reported from Спектр-2025; not re-checked);
    - a ministry proposal of 2026-10-28 to route all traffic of foreign domains through ТСПУ and cap it.
- **Applies to:** 2021–2026. **Sources:** S153, S076, S023, S124, S087, S042, S140, S085, S135, S118, S156. **Confidence:** high for official statements; medium for the rest.

#### 1.4 Filters have no 100G; balancer line rate above 160 bytes: updates
- **Claim:** filters connect at 10G, with no 100G interfaces; the balancer runs at line rate for packets above 160 bytes.
- **Newer sources:**
  - **Filters.** The vendor's current senior line (4080/4120/4160/5200, up to 200 Gbit/s) offers 1×/2×100GE cards. No source says whether project filters use them.
  - **Balancer.** The vendor page for the 3.2 Tbit/s balancer gives 4.7 Bpps. By arithmetic that is line rate down to about 65-byte packets, not 160.
- **Applies to:** 2024–2026. **Sources:** S062, S061, S055. **Confidence:** high for the vendor pages; low for project use and for the 160-byte figure.

### 2. traffic-flow (docs/traffic-flow.md)

#### 2.x Blocking actions on the path: updates
- **Claim:** a blocked HTTP request gets a redirect to a stub page and a blocked HTTPS connection gets a TCP Reset. The MPLS case is special: the filter waits for the reply and substitutes it.
- **Newer sources:**
  - **Reply substitution is the general method.** In 2022 the main HTTPS method rewrote server-to-client packets «by truncating the payload and changing the TCP flags to RST/ACK», keeping TTL, sequence and ack numbers. The substitution the chapter describes only for MPLS was therefore the method for ordinary traffic. No RST towards the server was reported.
  - **Dropping.** Other domains are dropped. In March–April 2026 TLS was blocked «through packet dropping for 2,644 domains».
  - **HTTP 307.** No measurement shows the stub redirect (see 10.1.5).
- **Applies to:** 2021–2026. **Sources:** S037, S043, S034, S036. **Confidence:** high for 2022; medium for 2026.

QUIC: see 17.4.9.

### 3. placement (docs/placement.md)

#### 3 / 3.2 Placement close to subscribers, before CGNAT preferred: confirms
- **Claim:** ТСПУ goes into the operator's links at one of three points; between BRAS and CGNAT is the most convenient.
- **Newer sources:**
  - **RKN's 2019 letter**, as quoted: «Типичная точка установки ТСПУ – после (ближе к магистрали) BRAS/BNG или, менее предпочтительно, после NAT. Установка до NAT желательна». The IMC 2022 paper cites this guideline as «before carrier-grade NAT, close to end users».
  - **Measured position:**
    - within the first three hops at all three home vantage points (2022);
    - within the first five hops, mobile included (2021).
- **Applies to:** 2019–2022. **Sources:** S070, S037, S034. **Confidence:** high.

#### 3 Official placement rules: updates (adds the legal basis)
- **Claim:** the chapter gives engineering reasons only.
- **Newer sources:**
  - **Law.** ТСПУ on equipment carrying more than 10 Gbit/s (ст. 46 п. 5.1) and at IXPs (ст. 56.2).
  - **Минцифры order № 44 (route).** User, then access node, then the node «через который проходит весь входящий и исходящий трафик абонентов, подключенных к одному узлу доступа», then ТСПУ, then the internet. An ISP using another operator's network goes through that operator's ТСПУ.
  - **RKN order № 25:**
    - the network must allow all traffic to pass through ТСПУ;
    - the management channel must be at least 100 Mbit/s and allow a VPN;
    - the UPS must hold at least 4 h.
  - **ПП 1333:**
    - ГРЧЦ chooses the place in its plan and asks for network schemes at least 90 days ahead;
    - ТСПУ capacity must match or exceed the network's.

  No act names BRAS or CGNAT, so the three points remain engineering practice.
- **Applies to:** 2021–2026. **Sources:** S031, S008, S017, S026. **Confidence:** high.

#### 3 (and 4.2) Both directions needed; asymmetric traffic goes to type B: updates
- **Claim:** ТСПУ тип А must see both directions of a session; asymmetric traffic at large operators' core is handled by type B.
- **Newer sources:**
  - «asymmetric routing is common in Russia».
  - Upstream-only ТСПУ devices were found at the first link of transit ISPs (Rostelecom, RasCom) and behind 417 echo servers in 15 ASes.
  - They block anyway, with drop-based SNI blocking, while seeing one direction. The result is under-blocking and over-blocking.
- **Applies to:** 2022. **Sources:** S037. **Confidence:** high (peer-reviewed, few vantage points).

#### 3 Data-centre and hosting networks: updates
- **Claim:** not covered; the chapter discusses subscriber networks.
- **Newer sources:**
  - **2022.** Rented data-centre VPS showed «little to no signs of censorship».
  - **2026.** Rented machines in Moscow, St Petersburg and Novosibirsk were censored, with the device between hop 3 and hop 4 in Moscow.
  - **2025 talk.** TSPU sits at "residential modems and data center connections".
  - **Hosting providers** must be in the RKN registry and use ЦМУ ССОП services (2024), and they take part in exercises.
- **Applies to:** 2022–2026. **Sources:** S037, S043, S039, S016, S031. **Confidence:** medium.

#### 3.1 Mobile networks (GTP-U before the gateway, Gi after it): no public source
No measurement distinguishes the two placements; see the open questions.

### 4. echelon (docs/echelon.md)

#### 4.1–4.2 A second echelon, «ТСПУ тип Б»: not confirmed publicly; the legal basis updates the picture
- **Claim:** a separate type-B system filters traffic at large operators' core and peering.
- **Newer sources:**
  - **No trace of the terms.** No public source uses «эшелон» or «тип Б».
  - **The law allows upstream filtering.** Since 2023 interconnecting operators must route connected ISPs' traffic through ТСПУ. Минцифры order № 44 lets legal entities' traffic pass through ТСПУ «установленные на разных узлах».
  - **Cross-border nodes:** 100% covered since August 2023; 86 such nodes in 2025.
  - **Measurement.** Upstream-only devices at transit ISPs block by SNI and IP while seeing one direction.
- **Applies to:** 2022–2025. **Sources:** S031, S008, S090, S119, S037. **Confidence:** medium.

#### 4.1 "Type A is installed at most operators": contradicts (as worded)
- **Newer sources:** 493 of 1,981 operators are connected to ТСПУ (RKN, 2025). See 1.3.
- **Applies to:** 2025. **Sources:** S107. **Confidence:** medium (explanatory note via Habr).

#### 4.3.1–4.3.2 IP/port lists by BGP, IP blocking at the balancer: updates (indirect)
- **Claim:** Eco Highway blocks by IP and port lists loaded by BGP from the centre.
- **Newer sources:**
  - **IP blocking was measured in 2022.** Packets from inside were dropped, and replies on connections opened from outside were rewritten to RST/ACK, «regardless of packet payload or TCP ports». It correlated with SNI blocking on upstream-only transit boxes.
  - **2025-03-20.** Cloudflare was unreachable for about 21 h on some Siberian ISPs.
  - **2026.** One Moscow AS dropped traffic to particular Cloudflare prefixes.

  BGP distribution of lists cannot be seen from outside.
- **Applies to:** 2022–2026. **Sources:** S037, S164, S172. **Confidence:** low to medium.

#### 4.3 / 4.4 Eco Highway on the Tofino platform: updates (supply)
- **Newer sources:**
  - Intel ended Tofino: last orders 2024-10-30, deliveries by 2025-02-28.
  - Intel suspended its Russian business in 2022.
  - Nothing public describes Eco Highway hardware or a successor. The domestic bypass still uses Tofino.
- **Applies to:** 2022–2025. **Sources:** S049, S082, S048. **Confidence:** high for the Intel facts; low for the effect on ТСПУ.

4.6 (no logging on Eco Highway): no public source.

### 5. bypass (docs/bypass.md)

#### 5.2 / 5.6 Silicom in the federal project; Silicom stopped support "from 2022": confirms in substance; the date is unverified
- **Newer sources:**
  - **Before.** In 2021 a Silicom bypass was part of the ТСПУ kit offered to operators.
  - **Silicom's 20-F for 2022** (filed 2023-04-27): the impact of the war and sanctions is «unknown».
  - **From the 20-F for 2023** (filed 2024-04-30, repeated for 2025): «we have ceased to do business with such sanctioned countries». No date is given.
- **Applies to:** 2021–2026. **Sources:** S074, S011, S020, S029. **Confidence:** high for the exit; low for the 2022 date.

#### 5.6 Domestic bypass (Булат, RDP software, Сигналтек hardware) "tested from 2023": updates (it is deployed)
- **Newer sources:**
  - **RDP manual.** RDP hosted «BYPASS SP100G4M Руководство пользователя» (May 2023). It describes EcoSwitch software, sends faults to «техподдержку ООО "БУЛАТ"» and places the device «в разрыв» of trunk links in front of EcoFilter farms.
  - **Сигналтек** (the real site is signaltec.ru, http only) sells the SP100G4M «СигналПасс» with «Tofino ASIC (16x100G)», developed and made in Russia.
  - **Deployment.** ГРЧЦ (June 2024): «Обходной переключатель поставляется с прошлого года… уже на несколько сотен узлов установлен».
  - **Modes in the manual:** auto, primary, tap (the default), software-bypass and physical-bypass. A state-on-failure setting chooses bypass or tap, and there is «force-up – режим, исключающий link-flap». What it does on power loss is not stated.
- **Applies to:** 2023–2024. **Sources:** S047, S048, S094. **Confidence:** high for the documents; medium that SP100G4M is the ТСПУ bypass.

#### 10.1.4 Recognition by tens of features; encrypted traffic recognised indirectly: updates

#### 23.7 Working with the operator: updates (formal procedure)
- **Claim:** practical cooperation with operators and subscribers; bypass switches agreed with the operator.
- **Newer sources:**
  - **ПП 1667 (from 2026-03-01).** Interaction goes through the RKN personal account or automated links with the ИС МУ ССОП:
    - requests for information on ТСПУ operation: reviewed in 5 working days, answered in 2 more, with «вывод о влиянии функционирования ТСПУ на работу сети связи оператора»; up to 20 working days through a commission;
    - claims: a commission, 20 working days;
    - п. 36: the operator may stop routing traffic through ТСПУ when a ТСПУ failure stops traffic (after posting in the account), or when traffic parameters do not match the project documentation or lawful resources are blocked (after posting and receiving an instruction). RKN reviews within 24 h.
  - **ПП 1333 duties:**
    - report incidents affecting ТСПУ to ЦМУ ССОП, exercises included;
    - give access within 48 h, or 24 h for emergency recovery;
    - report network changes within 7 working days;
    - give 90 days' notice before reconstruction or decommissioning, and at least 10 days before connecting another operator.

    ГРЧЦ warns the operator at least 7 working days before work at the site.
  - **Contacts.** ЦМУ ССОП's duty phone is on its portal.
  - **Limits on operators.** Operators have no access to ТСПУ management. They are not liable for licence breaches caused by ТСПУ failures.
- **Applies to:** 2025–2026. **Sources:** S028, S026, S032, S115, S031. **Confidence:** high (primary texts).
